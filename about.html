About Food Craft

We are students who train, study and wanted healthy food with honest numbers — so we built it.

Purpose

Healthy sport food, made simple

Food Craft shows the calories and protein of every meal, lets you build your own bowl and brings it to your door. No guessing, no calorie apps in another tab.

Who it is for: university students, gym-goers and busy people in Astana who want to eat better without spending hours planning.

  • Browse and filter sport meals with calories and protein
  • Search real recipes through a public API
  • Build a custom bowl and save it to your account
  • Order delivery to campus, EXPO or the Left Bank
